Chef's Table

An intimate bespoke multi-course journey at Gourmet Haven

Welcome to the pinnacle of **Gourmet Haven**. The Chef's Table is a restricted reservation-only experience limited to **8 select guests per evening**. You will sit directly in the heart of our culinary vault, interacting face-to-face with Executive Chef Lucien Hale as he orchestrates a bespoke tableside sensory tasting.

Amuse-Bouche

Golden Oyster Nectar

Freshly harvested cold-water oysters crowned with golden lemon verbena foam and toasted pine nut essence.

First Course

Imperial Caviar Medley

Osetra imperial caviar served on soft buck-wheat blinis with light lemon verbena whipped crème fraiche.

Second Course

Gold-Leaf Foie Gras

Pan-seared decadent artisan goose liver garnished with sweet caramelized baby figs and flakes of 24k gold leaf.

Main Voyage

L'Éclat de Turbot Rôti

Slow bone-roasted turbot basted in artisan butter, draped inside a velvet-like caviar and white champagne reduction.

Intermezzo

Emerald Sorbet Elixir

A refreshing, botanical cold-pressed palette cleanser made from garden mint, lime zest, and cold gin-infused sorbet.

Grand Finale

Madagascar Mille-Feuille

Crisped golden puff pastry sheets stacked between sweet ribbons of Madagascar vanilla diplomat cream.
