Privacy Policy

We value your trust and are fully committed to safeguarding your personal data

Last Updated: May 19, 2026

1. Information We Collect

We collect information directly from you when you interact with Gourmet Haven. This includes when you book a table reservations, place online delivery orders, contact our customer support, or sign up for newsletters.

The categories of personal data we collect include:

  • Contact details such as your full name, email address, physical shipping address, and phone number.
  • Dining and transaction information, including reservation dates, order history, and dish choices.
  • Secure payment simulation details (note: we do not store full raw credit card information on our servers).
  • Device information, IP addresses, and browsing statistics collected via cookies to optimize your user experience.

2. How We Use Your Data

We utilize your information strictly to support your premium dining experience:

  • Processing and managing your table reservations and seating configurations.
  • Managing checkout carts, logistics, and routing for your gourmet meal deliveries.
  • Personalizing your website interactions and remembering your account cart preferences.
  • Communicating order tracking updates and customer service resolutions.

3. Data Protection & Security

Gourmet Haven employs robust physical, administrative, and technological security protocols (including SSL encryption and secure payment gateways) designed to safeguard your data against unauthorized access, loss, or manipulation.

**We never sell, rent, trade, or share your personal details with third-party advertising brokers.** Your data is shared exclusively with necessary partners (such as direct delivery couriers or secure transaction processors) solely to fulfill your orders.

4. Your Rights & Choices

You hold complete control over your personal information:

  • You can refuse or clear browser cookies in your device settings, though some delivery cart operations might be restricted.
  • You may request a complete copy of the personal data we hold about you at any time.
  • You can request the permanent deletion of your customer records and subscription opt-outs by sending a message through our support channels.
